Gravity is the attraction between any two bodies that have mass. It is why an
apple falls to the ground and why the Moon stays near the Earth.

The pull is always **mutual**: the apple is pulled towards the Earth exactly as
the Earth is pulled towards the apple. Only the masses differ — the Earth is so
much heavier that it is the apple we see move.

How strong the force is depends on two things:

- **the masses**: the larger they are, the stronger the pull;
- **the distance**: the force weakens quickly with separation — double the
  distance and the force drops to a quarter.

This is Newton's law of universal gravitation: F = G · m₁ · m₂ / r².
